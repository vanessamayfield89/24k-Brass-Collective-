Who we are

A new luxury live band, for the world’s most exclusive events

24k Brass Collective is a new luxury live band, bringing together exceptional musicians, outstanding vocalists and a distinctive brass heavy sound for the world’s most exclusive events.

Created for luxury weddings, destination celebrations, corporate events, private parties and international occasions, 24k combines sousaphone, trumpet, trombone and saxophone, with drums, keyboards and powerful vocals to create a live experience that moves effortlessly from sophisticated to spectacular.

The collective may be new, but the experience behind it is anything but. The management team has over 10 years’ experience in the live music and events industry, while the musicians have performed alongside artists including P!nk, Calvin Harris, Take That, Joss Stone, Chaka Khan, Rag’n’Bone Man and Tony Hadley, and at major events including the FIFA World Cup 2022 and Glastonbury.

For the ultimate party experience, 24k can also transform into DJ Live, combining a professional DJ with a bespoke combination of live brass, percussion and vocals.

The line-up

Starting at an 8-piece ensemble, growing to fit your event

24K Brass Collective is an 8-piece ensemble—male and female vocalists, trumpet, saxophone, trombone, sousaphone, keys and drums.

For bigger events, percussion and extra vocalists can be added to bring that unforgettable experience.
